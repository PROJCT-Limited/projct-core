About

The performance

It traces how digital tools developed alongside sexuality: innovation and autonomy, desire and extraction.

The lecture format, told through Instagram Stories, began with Julio Correa in Mindy Seu's Lecture Performance course at Yale School of Art in Fall 2023. They developed it together.

The book

A Sexual History of the Internet (2025) is a 700+ page artist book. Designed by Laura Coombs, edited by Meg Miller, published via Metalabel.

Mindy Seu

Artist and researcher, born 1991 in California, based in Los Angeles and New York. Author of Cyberfeminism Index (2023), commissioned by Rhizome and premiered at the New Museum. Associate Professor, Design Media Arts, UCLA.
